Fund a real tree.

Not a vague donation. You pick a project — a reforestation project in Sabang, Palawan, part of the UNESCO World Heritage buffer zone. The price? Set by the NGO. Transparent. No middleman markup.

Your money splits: 90% goes directly to the NGO and their field team. Only 10% sustains TreeCodex. Farmers are paid 20% above local market wages, because fair pay means better forests.

03

A farmer puts it in the ground.

Meet the person who actually plants your tree. They're part of the NGO's field team — paid 20% above local market wages because fair compensation creates sustainable reforestation.

They walk to the GPS coordinates along the coast of Palawan, plant the Rhizophora propagule into the mudflat, then open TreeCodex and take a GPS-tagged photo. The app captures exact coordinates, timestamp, and photo. No faking it.

Verified. Yours forever.

The NGO reviews the GPS proof, checks the photo, approves it. The field team gets paid. Your tree gets a permanent unique ID: TC-2026-00412.

It shows up in your Tree Catalog — a visual portfolio of every tree you've funded. Tap any tree to see its GPS coordinates, photo history, CO₂ data, and every donor who co-funded it. Share it with a public URL anyone can visit.
