4. Tree Planting and Payments

4.1 How Funding Works

  • Tree prices are set by the NGO managing each project — TreeCodex does not set prices
  • Multiple users may co-fund a single tree (crowdfunding model)
  • A tree is assigned to a planter only after it is 100% funded

4.2 Revenue Split

  • 90% of each payment goes directly to the NGO and their field team. The NGO is responsible for compensating planters at fair wages (minimum 20% above local market rates)
  • 10% is retained by TreeCodex to maintain and operate the platform

4.3 Payment Processing

  • All payments are processed by Stripe. TreeCodex never stores your card details
  • Payments are made in fiat currency only — no cryptocurrency
  • All transactions are final. Refunds are issued only if a funded tree cannot be planted due to project cancellation, in which case funds will be credited to your account or refunded to your original payment method

4.4 Tree Verification

  • Each planted tree is verified through GPS-tagged photographic proof submitted by the planter and reviewed by the NGO
  • Verified trees receive a unique identifier (e.g., TC-2026-XXXXX) and a permanent public page
  • TreeCodex facilitates verification but does not guarantee the long-term survival of any individual tree, as trees are subject to natural conditions

5. Carbon Calculations
